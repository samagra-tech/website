THE PHILOSOPHY

Every startup begins on Page One.

Where the idea is raw, the energy is high, and the world still feels possible. Inspired by Jeff Bezos's Day 1 philosophy — the moment you slip into Day 2, you start dying slowly — this group exists to keep that page open.

Page One is a close-knit community of founders and solopreneurs in software and tech who refuse to coast. We meet regularly over coffee in Chennai to talk about what's working, what's not, and how to stay inventive even when the grind gets real.
